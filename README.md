# Nab

**Copy a to-do. It's on your list.**

Nab is a tiny checklist that lives in your Mac's menu bar. Copy anything that sounds like a task, from an email, a chat or your notes, and it lands in your list, sorted by category and ranked by priority.

- Copy a list and every line becomes its own task.
- Copy "sent Sarah the numbers" and the matching task is checked off.
- "Before Friday" becomes a due date. Tasks you ignore for a week fade away.
- Passwords are never read. Right-click the icon to pause anytime.

## Opening Nab the first time

Nab isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once:

1. Drag Nab into Applications and open it. macOS shows a warning: click **Done**.
2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Nab.
3. Confirm with Touch ID or your password. Nab appears in your menu bar.

## Privacy

Short text that could be a task is sent to an AI to sort it. Passwords from password managers, and anything without a space (links, codes), are never sent. Nothing you copy is stored on the server, and your checklist stays on your Mac.
